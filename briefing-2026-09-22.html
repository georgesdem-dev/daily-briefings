☢️ Parliament Files NPT-Withdrawal Bill — Now Linked to Weapons Testing

Iranian lawmaker Hossein-Ali Haji Deligani confirmed a bill has been submitted to Parliament's Presiding Board seeking Iran's withdrawal from the Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty, filed with triple urgency and pending Speaker Ghalibaf's approval before an open-session debate. Unlike earlier withdrawal proposals, this draft's most consequential feature is new language explicitly tying NPT exit to nuclear-weapons testing rather than treaty exit alone. Lawmakers cited the IAEA Board of Governors' recent referral of Iran's file to the UN Security Council as justification, arguing "remaining in the NPT brings us nothing but losses."

This is the most significant escalation-risk signal of the week — a formal legislative move toward weapons-testing language, not just treaty withdrawal, would mark a major shift from the diplomatic-pressure track the war has followed since the April ceasefire.

🌟 Top Story: Microsoft's Biggest-Ever Patch Tuesday — 974 CVEs, 2 Exploited Zero-Days FEDERAL DEADLINE TODAY

September's Patch Tuesday is the largest single-day CVE release Microsoft has ever published — 974 vulnerabilities, including 723 in Windows and 222 in Office (111 of those in Office 2016). Two are confirmed exploited zero-days: CVE-2026-85880, a heap buffer overflow in Windows ALPC (CVSS 7.8) letting a local attacker gain SYSTEM privileges — only the second ALPC zero-day since January 2023 — and CVE-2026-81963, a link-following flaw in the Windows Update Stack, the first of seven such Update Stack elevation-of-privilege bugs patched since 2022 to be exploited in the wild. Twenty of the newly fixed flaws are wormable — remotely exploitable without authentication or user interaction. CISA added both zero-days to its KEV catalog, and the deadline for Federal Civilian Executive Branch agencies to patch is today, September 22.
