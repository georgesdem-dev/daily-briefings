🕳️ CVE-2026-88779: Citrix NetScaler SAML Zero-Day — Third Wave, Actively Exploited
Days after Citrix patched two prior zero-days (CVE-2026-88771/72), a third critical vulnerability hit the same product line. CVE-2026-88779 is a remotely triggerable memory overflow in NetScaler appliances configured as SAML Service Providers or Identity Providers (CVSS 8.7). Unauthenticated attackers can cause denial-of-service, effectively knocking SAML-based SSO offline — a significant risk for enterprises using NetScaler as an authentication gateway. CISA added it to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog with a federal remediation deadline. Citrix released patches in versions 14.1-73.41 and 13.1-64.28. Organizations using NetScaler for SAML should treat this as emergency-priority patching.

🐼 China APT "Warp Panda" Exploited VMware ESXi as Zero-Day — US Law Firms Compromised
Security researchers revealed that the China-nexus threat actor dubbed "Warp Panda" has been exploiting CVE-2026-41709, a seemingly low-severity VMware ESXi logging flaw, as a zero-day to reduce audit trail visibility at compromised organisations. Victims include US law firms and several unnamed US organisations. The tactic is notable: by exploiting insufficient logging, Warp Panda can perform privileged ESXi operations without generating audit records — making forensic reconstruction extremely difficult. The approach follows a broader Chinese APT playbook of maintaining long-dwell access with minimal operational noise, reminiscent of the ARMORY CASTLE intrusion pattern documented earlier this month.

💀 Spikey Scorpius Ransomware Wave — PAN-OS GlobalProtect CVE-2026-0257 as Entry Vector
A ransomware operator tracked as "Spikey Scorpius" is actively exploiting CVE-2026-0257, a critical authentication bypass in Palo Alto Networks PAN-OS GlobalProtect portals and gateways, to gain initial access without valid credentials. Once inside, attackers establish persistent VPN sessions, bypass multi-factor authentication, and conduct lateral movement. Sectors hit include education, healthcare, and government. The vulnerability allows attackers to bypass MFA entirely — one of the highest-impact authentication failure scenarios. Organisations should prioritise patching GlobalProtect portals immediately and hunt for anomalous VPN sessions established without proper credential chains.
