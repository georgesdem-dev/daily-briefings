🌟 Top Story: "Black October" Closes With a Chainalysis Breach Blamed on "Offensive Agentic" AI Techniques AI-DRIVEN ATTACK · NAMES, EMAILS, USER IDS EXPOSED

Blockchain analytics firm Chainalysis confirmed that an external actor used "offensive agentic techniques" to gain unauthorized access to its internal tools, exposing customer user IDs, full names, email addresses and organization role labels. The company said investigative data and passwords were untouched, and stressed the breach is unrelated to the record $6.36 billion Binance hack it has been helping trace this week — a notable irony, given Chainalysis's role tracking crypto crime. The term "offensive agentic techniques" implies an AI system capable of carrying out multi-step attack tasks with limited human direction was used to execute the intrusion, though Chainalysis has disclosed no further detail on the specific tooling, exploited vulnerabilities, or number of affected customers. The disclosure caps a month researchers are already calling "Black October" — a stretch marked by AI-accelerated zero-day exploitation and software supply-chain poisoning hitting core web runtimes, cloud platforms and developer toolchains, including this week's Ivanti, Cisco SD-WAN and Cisco ASA/FTD zero-days. Separately, Apple sent a fresh wave of mercenary-spyware threat notifications to iPhone users in 99 countries on October 30 — including journalists and documentary filmmakers — in a campaign researchers say is distinct from August's notifications and linked to new attack activity, arriving just as the multilateral Pall Mall Process nears final guidelines on commercial spyware use.
