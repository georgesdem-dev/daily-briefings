🎉 Chips & AI Names Lead a Sharp Holiday-Session Rebound

Friday's shortened Black Friday session (NYSE and Nasdaq closed at 1:00 PM ET) produced a decisive bounce in the names battered earlier in the week by tariff and inflation fears. Top individual gainers included Sandisk (+15.96%), Coherent (+14.07%), Kinetik Holdings (+9.25%), Astera Labs (+8.67%), Coeur Mining (+8.60%), TTM Technologies (+8.58%), Rocket Lab (+8.21%) and Lumentum Holdings (+7.98%) — memory, storage and optical names most directly tied to the AI infrastructure buildout. The result: the Dow's best week since 2022, and the S&P's best week since April 2025 (+5.6%).

Caution flag: analysts widely noted that a three-and-a-half-hour holiday session on thin volume doesn't give a clean read on conviction — Monday's full session is the one to watch for confirmation that this bounce has legs.

🛍️ Amazon Rides Record Black Friday Spending

AMZN jumped +3.96% to $254.12 as early holiday data showed consumers spending more online than expected: Thanksgiving Day online spending hit $7.1B, with Black Friday online sales projected near $12.9B. Amazon's "Black Friday Week" deal event (running through Nov 30) reinforced the retail segment's role as a second profit engine alongside AWS — relevant given JPMorgan's fresh $365 price target published this week on AWS growth acceleration.

🧠 Trusted Investor Insights MACRO VIEWS
Goldman Sachs Trading Desk

Warning: Another $100B of Global Equity Selling Expected Next Week

Even as the market posted its best week in years, Goldman's trading desk flagged roughly $70B of global equity sales in the past week and estimated around $100B more coming in the week ahead. That's the single most important counterweight to Friday's rally: thin holiday-session gains can be fragile, and systematic/rebalancing flows into early December could test this bounce quickly.

Goldman Sachs Asset Management

2027 Outlook: Growth Decelerates But Stays Supportive of Equities

Goldman's house view sees US growth near 2.4-2.5% in 2027 (above 1% in Europe and Japan), with 2026's exceptional earnings growth — boosted by AI investment and higher energy prices — decelerating next year but remaining a positive backdrop for equities. They continue to favor broadening earnings and themes around AI, defense and economic security, reinforcing the case for diversification beyond the mega-cap AI names.

Howard Marks / Oaktree Capital (standing view)

A Fourth Reversal in a Volatile Month — Still Price Discovery, Not Panic

Friday's broad, all-sector bounce is the fourth distinct directional swing in a single week (+31%, -5.9%, -3.9% intraday reversal, now a sharp rebound) for the AI-chip complex alone. Marks' framework holds: this remains a market working out fair value for AI infrastructure spend under new tariff and inflation uncertainty, not a market in freefall. The discipline — quality, income, valuation — doesn't change because one session was green.

Warren Buffett / Berkshire Hathaway

No New Public Commentary — Record Cash Still on the Sidelines

Still no fresh statement since the correction began in early November. Goldman's own warning about more selling to come next week is exactly the kind of setup Buffett's cash position is built to capitalize on if it materializes.
