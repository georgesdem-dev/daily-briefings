🌟 Top Story: Tanker Strikes Near Daily Pace as Diplomacy Stalls Again MT LIPSI

UK Maritime Trade Operations logged at least four vessels struck by unidentified projectiles in and around the Strait of Hormuz over the past week, with the pace of successful strikes approaching one tanker a day. No ceasefire is currently in effect.

🌟 Top Story: Critical FortiMail Zero-Day Still Unpatched for Every Branch as Exploitation Continues CVE-2026-104286 · CVSS 9.8

CISA's federal remediation deadline for the critical Fortinet FortiMail flaw passed October 4 with Fortinet still not shipping fixed builds for every affected branch, leaving administrators relying on workarounds rather than a permanent patch. CVE-2026-104286 allows an unauthenticated attacker to write arbitrary files on the underlying system via crafted HTTP or HTTPS requests, pairing a classic path-handling flaw with a NULL-byte trick that lets attackers smuggle a malicious path past filters. Separately, Citrix NetScaler administrators are dealing with a sixth exploited vulnerability of the year: CISA added CVE-2026-88779 (CVSS 8.7) to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on October 4, giving federal agencies until October 7 to act. Security firm WatchTowr reproduced the bug and clarified it's a denial-of-service flaw used to crash NetScaler appliances already patched against two earlier flaws (CVE-2026-88771/88772) — apparently to speed up exploitation of those other bugs rather than as a standalone attack path.

If you run FortiMail, apply Fortinet's interim mitigations now — there is no complete patch yet for all branches. NetScaler admins have until October 7 to remediate CVE-2026-88779 per CISA's KEV deadline.
