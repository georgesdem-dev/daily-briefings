🌟 Top Story: Rackspace Confirms Spot Cloud Breach Let Attackers Read Customer Kubernetes Secrets LEAKED CREDENTIALS · SERVICE RESTORED NOV 21

Rackspace disclosed that an unauthorized party used leaked credentials to access parts of its Spot cloud platform on November 17 (roughly 00:20-22:00 UTC), with the company confirming the intrusion on November 18 after a customer reported suspicious activity. Rackspace said attackers were able to act as legitimate users within some customer organizations — creating pods in Kubernetes clusters, reading Kubernetes Secrets (including database credentials, API keys, and TLS certificates), and listing nodes and volumes — before full service restoration was announced on November 21. Rackspace published indicators of compromise, including source IP 23.224.79.106 and the range 23.224.0.0/15, plus suspicious pod/object name prefixes ("i1", "glm-", "probe"), and is advising affected customers to reissue API tokens and rotate all credentials stored in their clusters. Separately, Chinese-nexus hackers reportedly used Cairn AI agents to autonomously discover and exploit a zero-day (CVE-2026-133968) in MONAI Label, an open-source medical-imaging annotation tool — an early real-world example of AI-agent-driven vulnerability discovery against healthcare infrastructure. Microsoft also warned that attackers exploited a flaw in its Entra Connect Health service to obtain administrator email addresses and tenant configuration data, the FBI is investigating a reported fresh breach of its National Crime Information Center (NCIC) database, and the BADBOX malware family has returned to large-scale ad-fraud operations on Android TV boxes, now generating a reported 40 billion daily ad requests. New, as-yet-unexploited PAN-OS NGINX flaws were also disclosed, and CISA added Cisco Expressway, SAP NetWeaver, Broadcom VMware vCenter, and Apache Roller flaws to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog this week.
