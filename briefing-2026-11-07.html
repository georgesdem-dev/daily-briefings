🌟 Top Story: SonicWall SAML/MFA Bypass Added to CISA's KEV Catalog as October Ransomware Tally Hits 855 Incidents CVE-2026-19868 · ACTIVELY EXPLOITED

CISA added CVE-2026-19868, a SAML authentication and MFA bypass affecting SonicWall devices, to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, confirming active exploitation in the wild and giving federal agencies a mandated remediation deadline. The disclosure lands amid a grim monthly tally from ZeroFox: 855 ransomware and extortion incidents recorded in October, with manufacturing the hardest-hit sector at 151 incidents. Separately, the Interlock ransomware group has been linked to exploitation of a Cisco Firepower Management Center zero-day (CVE-2026-20131), giving attackers a path into FMC-managed security infrastructure itself — a notable escalation since FMC is the control plane for Cisco firewall deployments.
