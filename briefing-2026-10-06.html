🌟 Top Story: Economic Squeeze Deepens as Iran Loads Zero Oil Cargoes in September RIAL AT RECORD LOW

US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said Iran loaded no crude oil cargoes onto tankers throughout all of September, confirming the US naval blockade — now in its second round since July 14 — has effectively halted Iranian crude exports. The pressure is visibly biting: the Iranian rial collapsed to roughly 2.688 million to the dollar, inflation is reported above 70%, and national security adviser Mohsen Rezaei told a government meeting on October 3, attended by President Pezeshkian, that the country has entered "one of its most difficult periods." President Pezeshkian accepted the resignation of petroleum minister Mohsen Paknejad, replacing him with acting minister Hamid Board, who described the situation as a "full-scale economic war" and vowed to maximize production and exports through new, unspecified strategies. Despite the pressure, Tehran shows no sign of folding on the core disputes: officials maintain "Hormuz will not be opened by threats or pressure," and Pezeshkian says Iran has adopted a "new arrangement" to manage the sanctions regime rather than concede to it. A CNN analysis published today captures the stalemate bluntly: Trump has backed Iran into a corner, but the regime isn't blinking. Tanker traffic through the Strait of Hormuz remains suppressed, with dozens of Iranian vessels reportedly stranded as crews fear capture by US and allied forces.

🌟 Top Story: New Citrix NetScaler Zero-Day Can Knock SAML Deployments Offline CVE-2026-88779 · CVSS 8.7

Citrix NetScaler's rough month continues: a new zero-day, CVE-2026-88779, is being actively exploited just days after two other flaws (CVE-2026-88771/88772) were patched. The bug is a memory-overflow issue affecting NetScaler ADC and Gateway instances configured as a SAML service provider or identity provider, and security firm WatchTowr confirmed it's a denial-of-service flaw that crashes appliances rather than granting code execution — but attackers appear to be using the resulting crashes to accelerate exploitation of the other two NetScaler bugs. The Register notes this is the sixth exploited NetScaler vulnerability of 2026 added to CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, with federal agencies given until October 7 to remediate. Separately, the critical FortiMail flaw covered in recent days (CVE-2026-104286, CVSS 9.8, unauthenticated arbitrary file writes) blew past its October 4 federal remediation deadline with Fortinet still lacking a complete patch for every affected branch — agencies and enterprises remain on interim workarounds only.
