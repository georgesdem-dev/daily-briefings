🌟 Top Story: Critical Deserialization Flaw in Radius Booking Plugin Leads a Routine Disclosure Day CVSS 9.8 · WORDPRESS ECOSYSTEM

Today's disclosures were dominated by WordPress-ecosystem plugin flaws rather than a single marquee incident. CVE-2026-119516, a critical-severity insecure deserialization bug (CVSS 9.8) in the Radius Booking calendar plugin, tops the list, alongside CVE-2026-87964, a high-severity remote code execution flaw (CVSS 7.2) in the Product Feed Manager for WooCommerce. Several medium-severity issues were also disclosed, including a cross-site scripting bug (CVE-2026-65022) and an account-lockout weakness (CVE-2026-66987). None of today's CVEs have confirmed ransomware campaigns tied to them yet, and the Radius Booking flaw has not been added to CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog as of this briefing — but a CVSS 9.8 deserialization bug on a widely installed booking plugin is the kind of vulnerability that typically draws mass scanning within days of disclosure.
