🌟 Top Story: Citrix Ships NetScaler SAML Patch Just Ahead of Today's CISA Deadline CVE-2026-88779 · PATCHED

Citrix released a fix for the NetScaler SAML memory-overflow zero-day (CVE-2026-88779, CVSS 8.7) hours ahead of today's CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities remediation deadline, closing out the sixth exploited NetScaler flaw added to the KEV catalog in 2026. Security teams that patched in time avoid the crash-looping behavior that attackers had been using to mask exploitation of two earlier NetScaler bugs. The relief is partial: FortiMail's critical unauthenticated-file-write flaw (CVE-2026-104286, CVSS 9.8) remains without a complete vendor patch across all affected branches, three days past its own October 4 federal remediation deadline, leaving agencies and enterprises on interim workarounds only.
