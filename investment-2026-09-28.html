📊 Market Snapshot LIVE DATA

📉 Futures Slip as Oil Jumps on Rejected Iran Deal

US stock futures fell in overnight trading as oil prices climbed after Trump rejected Iran's proposal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz. Dow futures are down 0.30%, S&P 500 futures -0.33%, and Nasdaq-100 futures -0.60% in early trading. Brent crude (Nov contract) is at $106.65 and WTI at $93.98, both up sharply from Friday's pullback, denting some of the optimism that lifted markets into the weekend.

🏦 Fed Hike Odds Jump — 70% for October, 95% for December

The CME FedWatch Tool now shows traders pricing in a nearly 70% chance of a rate hike in October and a 95% probability of another in December — a notably more hawkish setup than the market was pricing just a week ago. This is arguably a bigger structural risk than the day's oil headlines and helps explain why gold is falling even as geopolitical tension rises.

🤖 AI Stocks Keep Carrying the Market

Microsoft jumped roughly 3.7% Friday, and Meta rallied 16.8% over the week after unveiling "Meta Muse," a new consumer AI agent that's generated strong investor enthusiasm. Applied Materials (+2.3%) and Lam Research (+2.6%) also gained as chip-equipment names rode the AI-strength wave. This continued AI leadership is the main offsetting force against the oil/rate headwinds this week.

Watching the Collision of Higher Rates and Consumer Strain
