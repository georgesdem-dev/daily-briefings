🌟 Top Story: Federal Remediation Deadline Hits Today for BeyondTrust and Fortinet KEV Entries CVE-2026-102764 · CISA DEADLINE TODAY

Today, October 19, is CISA's federal remediation deadline for CVE-2026-102764, the BeyondTrust Remote Support zero-day (CVSS 9.3) that a China-linked espionage group spent roughly seven weeks quietly exploiting before detection, and for a newly added Fortinet vulnerability placed on the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog alongside it. Both flaws now require Federal Civilian Executive Branch agencies to have applied fixes as of today. The Fortinet addition lands alongside fresh reporting on PEERSTITCH, a newly identified malware family deployed via a FortiOS flaw that bypasses security checks specifically on sessions tagged as VPN traffic — a technique that lets attackers blend into expected remote-access patterns rather than triggering typical anomaly detection. Separately, SecurityWeek reports that Plex2Shell, a four-vulnerability exploit chain targeting Plex Media Server, was weaponized by attackers within hours of technical details and a working exploit going public, underscoring how little buffer defenders now get between disclosure and real-world exploitation for self-hosted media and remote-access software alike.

Confirm today whether BeyondTrust Remote Support and the newly listed Fortinet product are patched in your environment — both are now past their federal KEV deadline, and PEERSTITCH's VPN-session evasion technique means tagged-VPN traffic should not be assumed safe by default.
