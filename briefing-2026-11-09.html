🌟 Top Story: SonicWall SAML/MFA Bypass Confirmed Exploited, Joins CISA's KEV with a Nov 20 Deadline CVE-2026-19868 · FEDERAL REMEDIATION DUE NOV 20

SonicWall confirmed active exploitation of CVE-2026-19868, an authentication-enforcement bug that lets attackers with valid credentials bypass SAML-enforced MFA on SonicOS SSL-VPN logins. CISA added it to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, giving US civilian federal agencies until November 20 to remediate. SonicWall SSL-VPN appliances carry a troubled history — Akira ransomware affiliates compromised them even with OTP-based MFA enabled back in 2025 — making this bypass a high-value target for ransomware crews. Separately, Sega Corporation formally disclosed that an unauthorized third party exfiltrated 2,196,044 customer newsletter email addresses, tracing the intrusion to a pair of critical PHP core memory-safety flaws dubbed "PHP2Shell" (CVE-2026-109618/109619). Anubis ransomware operators were also reported deploying a new custom backdoor called "Lindnor" alongside 13 security-evasion tools in an October intrusion against a US organization.
