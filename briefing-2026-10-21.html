🚨 Critical Advisory

FBI/CISA: SafePay Ransomware Hits 600+ Critical Infrastructure Orgs — Destroys Backups

A joint advisory issued October 20 by the FBI, CISA, Secret Service, and international partners confirms SafePay ransomware has breached more than 600 critical infrastructure organizations across North America, South America, Europe, and Australia since August 2024. The group factory-resets NAS devices, deletes all shadow copies, and terminates backup agents before encrypting — leaving victims with zero recovery paths. Initial access vectors include phishing, brute-force, and exploitation of CVE-2024-40711 and CVE-2024-40766 on edge devices. Operators also use AI-assisted lateral movement.
