🇨🇳 BeyondTrust CVE-2026-102764 (CVSS 9.3): China APT ARMORY CASTLE — Seven Weeks of Silent Access
What happened: BeyondTrust disclosed a critical authentication-bypass vulnerability (CVE-2026-102764) in its Remote Support appliances on October 15–16. Google Threat Intelligence Group (GTIG) and Mandiant attribute active exploitation to ARMORY CASTLE (also tracked as UNC5221 / WARP PANDA), a China-nexus espionage actor. The zero-day was first exploited on August 29 — seven weeks before disclosure — against one BeyondTrust-hosted instance and one self-hosted appliance.

Technical details: CVSS 9.3. The flaw is an authentication bypass by spoofing: internal appliance components fail to verify message origin, allowing unauthenticated remote attackers to execute arbitrary commands. Affected versions: 24.3.1 through 26.2.3. Patch BT26-05-RS is available; SaaS instances were silently patched October 8.

Post-compromise pattern: Harvest stored credentials → exfiltrate organizational data for reconnaissance → deploy custom persistent tooling → delete forensic artifacts. CISA added to KEV on October 16 with mandatory remediation deadline.
