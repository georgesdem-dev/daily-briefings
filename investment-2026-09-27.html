📊 Market Snapshot WEEKEND WRAP + WEEK AHEAD

🚫 Trump Rejects Iran's Hormuz Roadmap — Deal Hopes Stall

Iran's proposal called for the US to lift its naval blockade, unfreeze some Iranian assets, and ease sanctions on Iranian oil exports in exchange for reopening the Strait of Hormuz and resuming nuclear talks. Trump rejected it Saturday as unacceptable, saying Iran wants to reopen the corridor immediately only because "they are losing so badly." Iran's FM Araghchi says Tehran has only seen the rejection via media reports, not an official channel — leaving the door technically open, but the fast-resolution scenario markets priced Friday now looks less likely for Monday's open.

🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia Escalates Diplomatic Pressure

Saudi Arabia has called on UN member states to join efforts defending freedom of navigation in Middle East waterways, framing the standoff as a threat to global energy security — a sign regional players are increasingly worried about a prolonged shipping disruption rather than a quick resolution.

📅 Week Ahead (Sep 28 – Oct 2): Payrolls, Yields, and AI Strength

Last week: S&P +1.2%, Nasdaq +2.1%, Dow +0.3%, Russell 2000 -0.8% — a resilient week that shrugged off the mid-week yield spike, helped by continued strength in AI-related mega-caps. This week's key risk: if Treasury yields keep climbing at the recent pace, or Friday's Nonfarm Payrolls print comes in well above the 4.1% unemployment-rate estimate, it could tip the week negative. The 30-year fixed mortgage rate has already jumped to 7.45%, its highest in more than two years — a growing drag on consumers even as headline indices sit near record highs.
