🌟 Top Story: N-able Patches 17 More N-central Zero-Days as a 3-Day CISA Deadline Hits Check Point's Gaia Root Flaw 17 NEW CVES · LEGACY APIS KILLED

N-able patched 17 additional N-central vulnerabilities (CVE-2026-129271 through CVE-2026-129287) dropped as zero-days and permanently disabled legacy APIs to cut off in-the-wild exploitation — covering privilege escalation, predictable passcodes, command execution, missing auth, audit-log tampering, user enumeration, XXE, SSRF and RCE flaws, a direct follow-on from last week's mass N-central disclosure. Separately, CISA added CVE-2026-123039, a Check Point Gaia OS root-access flaw exploited via the "PHP2Shell" technique, to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on November 13, giving federal civilian agencies only three days — until November 16 — to remediate. Rapid7's weekly roundup flags CVE-2026-90436 (CVSS 9.8) as now confirmed to affect both Atlassian Confluence Data Center and N-able N-central via a shared Axis library, plus newly cataloged flaws in Cisco BroadWorks (XXE) and two Barracuda products (command injection and auth bypass). A month-long campaign has also hit over 40 MSP ScreenConnect servers exploiting CVE-2026-84869 (CVSS 9.9) to silently push a rogue ScreenConnect client via a Telegram-bot-controlled batch file, with no prompt shown to the technician.
