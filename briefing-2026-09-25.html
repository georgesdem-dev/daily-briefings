🌟 Top Story: Cisco Firewall Manager Flaws Hit by Nation-State and Ransomware Actors CVSS 10.0

Cisco Talos confirmed three distinct threat clusters actively exploiting two patched Secure Firewall Management Center (FMC) vulnerabilities: CVE-2026-20079, a maximum-severity (CVSS 10.0) authentication bypass letting an unauthenticated attacker execute scripts and gain root, and CVE-2026-20316, a static-credential flaw enabling login via a low-privileged account. One cluster deployed a Cyclops Blink variant — malware previously attributed to Russia's Sandworm APT — via reverse shells and proxy tooling. A separate cluster, assessed with high confidence as a ransomware affiliate, used the static-credential bug for reconnaissance and credential harvesting consistent with Qilin ransomware TTPs. CISA added CVE-2026-20079 to its KEV catalog with a Sept 12 federal patch deadline that has now passed.

🚨 WSO2 API Manager JWT Bypass Added to CISA's KEV Catalog CVE-2026-5430 · CVSS 9.8

A cryptographic-signature verification flaw in WSO2 API Manager, API Control Plane, Traffic Manager, and Universal Gateway lets attackers forge admin JWT tokens by signing with an unsupported algorithm, leading to full account takeover. WatchTowr honeypots captured active exploitation attempts from Sept 13 — five months after WSO2's original May 3 advisory — with attackers extracting consumer keys/secrets and compromising entire API ecosystems. Separately, an unauthenticated SQL-injection flaw in Roundcube Webmail (CVE-2026-48842) is also under exploitation.

🌟 Top Story: Microsoft Details EvilTokens — a Cybercrime Platform Run by an AI Chatbot AI-NATIVE CRIME

Microsoft published new detail on the EvilTokens phishing-as-a-service operation it disrupted this month, describing it as a cybercrime platform that used AI at every step of the attack chain — not just for delivery, but for decision-making. At its center was an AI-style chatbot that analyzed a victim's compromised inbox to identify trusted relationships, payment authorizations, and other circumstances where fraud was most likely to succeed, then drafted the social-engineering messages itself. The disruption uncovered 8,708 unique victim accounts across 6,585 corporate domains in 79 countries; London's Metropolitan Police arrested two men, aged 32 and 38, on Sept 11. Microsoft says large portions of the toolkit itself were AI-assisted in development — a case study in how AI is lowering the skill floor for scaled, targeted fraud.

This is the clearest evidence yet that AI-driven target selection and message generation, not just AI-written phishing copy, are now operational realities in commodity cybercrime — relevant context alongside yesterday's UN Security Council warnings from Altman and Amodei.
Sources: The Hacker News
🏢 Dataiku Launches Agent Management to Rein In Enterprise AI Agent Sprawl

Dataiku announced Agent Management, a standalone product that discovers every AI agent an enterprise is running across AWS Bedrock, Databricks, Google Vertex, Microsoft Copilot Studio/Azure Foundry, Salesforce Agentforce, Snowflake Cortex, and Dataiku itself (plus custom environments via OpenTelemetry), tracks their business and technical performance, and assigns each a risk tier based on autonomy level, data sensitivity, and potential business impact. High-risk agents — those touching customers, sensitive data, or live transactions — get standing certification records and scheduled re-testing. General availability is planned for October, priced per instance annually with metered monitoring. The launch responds directly to a problem Dataiku itself flagged: "every enterprise is building agents; almost none can account for them."
