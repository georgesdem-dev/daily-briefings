⚠️ Key Risks & Watchlist RISK MONITOR
🪖 Geopolitical Risks
  • No confirmed durable Iran ceasefire — oil's slide reflects hope, not a finalized deal
  • Strait of Hormuz remains a persistent chokepoint; any renewed disruption spikes oil sharply
  • Fast-unwinding oil risk premium is itself a risk: XOM and energy broadly could give back gains quickly on de-escalation, or spike again on any flare-up
💰 Macro / Fed Risks
  • Fed policy path remains a genuine source of uncertainty this cycle — conflicting signals on rate direction into year-end warrant caution on rate-sensitive names
  • 10Y yield holding near 4.9%, still a headwind for growth-stock valuations
  • September/October historically volatile months for equities even after a strong run
📉 Earnings / Sector Risks
  • NVDA under DOJ scrutiny over the Groq chip-licensing deal — regulatory overhang on the AI trade
  • MSFT capex growing faster than revenue is the core bear argument
  • JNJ talc litigation remains unresolved despite strong fundamentals
  • UPS: BofA flagged Amazon-volume headwinds as a risk to watch into Q3 earnings
🔒 Portfolio-Specific Watch Items
  • NEE: Dominion Energy merger (~$66B) still needs regulatory approval

  • NVDA: HBM3e/CoWoS packaging capacity fully allocated through mid-2027 — a supply constraint, not demand
  • Watch for AI-trade breadth: is the rally broadening (Meta's Muse) or still narrowly chip-led?
